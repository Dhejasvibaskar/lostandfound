Welcome to the Lost & Found page

This is a very simple website where you can add lost items, add items you found, and browse everything that has been reported.

Everything is stored only in your browser using LocalStorage. If you clear the browser data, the items will be removed.

Report Lost Item Report Found Item

Quick steps

1. Go to “Report Lost” or “Report Found” and fill in the form.

2. Open “View Items” to search for items by type, category or text.

3. Click a card to see more details and contact information.
